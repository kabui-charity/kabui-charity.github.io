Operational Risk & Root Cause Analysis

Manufacturing Downtime Root Cause Analysis

Tracing recurring losses on a Kenyan production floor back to their actual driver.

Problem

Unplanned downtime and recurring defects quietly erode a manufacturer's margins, but without root cause analysis, teams tend to treat symptoms instead of what's actually driving the losses.

Approach

Used Python to analyse production and operations data from a Kenyan manufacturing context, isolating the recurring root causes behind downtime and defect rates rather than just reporting the numbers.

Python Root Cause Analysis Operations Data

Credit Risk & Fraud Analysis

Wekeza Analytics — Kenya Fintech BI Suite

Surfacing loan risk and irregular transaction activity across a fintech's lending and payment channels.

Problem

A fintech operating across mobile money, agency banking and digital lending needed visibility into which loans were turning risky and which transaction patterns looked irregular, before either became a loss.

Approach

Built a Tableau BI suite on a seven-table relational dataset, with a loan risk view tracking default rate, portfolio at risk and days-late by segment and county, alongside transaction and channel views built to surface unusual activity.

Tableau SQL Credit Risk Analysis
